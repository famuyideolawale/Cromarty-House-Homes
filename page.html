Buying off-plan

Reserve now, complete on handover

Early buyers secure their choice of floor and aspect at launch pricing. Each reservation helps fund the conversion, which is why the first homes are released before work begins.

  1. 1
    Choose a flat

    Compare floor plans, the 3D model and the walk-through, then shortlist your favourites.

  2. 2
    Reserve

    A reservation fee takes the flat off the market while your solicitor reviews the title and specification.

  3. 3
    Conclude missives

    Under Scots conveyancing, the contract is formed when missives are concluded and the deposit is paid.

  4. 4
    Complete on handover

    The balance is due when the flat is finished and signed off. Then move in or let it out.
